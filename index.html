How I approach engineering

I began with a foundation in IT, software development and system analysis. That combination taught me to look beyond the code: understand the requirement, consider the people using the system, and think about how the solution fits into a wider business process.

My early IT experience gave me practical grounding. At SigniFlow, I developed further as a core software engineer, working on application features and integrations in a business-critical product environment. I then moved into engineering leadership at SMSFlow, where the work extends beyond implementation to technical decisions, delivery, code quality, team support and production-minded problem-solving.

Alongside my engineering journey, my business-analysis work has strengthened the bridge between stakeholder needs and technical execution. My studies in China, including Mandarin and systems analysis, also helped shape my communication skills and my comfort working across different perspectives.

Today, I'm interested in opportunities where I can contribute hands-on technically, take ownership of delivery, mentor engineers, and keep growing in .NET, cloud architecture and system design.
